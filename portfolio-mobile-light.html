I'm a product designer from Accra, Ghana, and I never stop learning.

My work comes down to one idea: Fluency Without Learning. If you have to stop and figure out how something works, I haven't done my job yet. Most of what I design is for rural and low-literacy contexts, products that should make sense the first time you use them. I also design websites, this one included, and do some coding, mostly building with AI.

Before my national service, I designed web and mobile products at Soia Finance, a fintech startup in Accra, working with its founders and engineers. I'm now open to new roles, in Accra or remote.

Outside of work, I follow the Knicks, watch a lot of anime and can't choose between Nolan and Scorsese as my favourite director. I'm a little introverted, but I'm working on it.
