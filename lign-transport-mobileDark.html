Lign

Institutional Transport management, made legible and easy to integrate.

Introduction

Lign is a bus system for schools and workplaces. Each rider signs in with an ID from their own institution, then buys tickets and hops on buses run for their community. Think of it as a trotro personalised to one institution. I started it as a student and designed it as my final year project. It began with student commutes. It is built to fit any school, hospital, factory or company with a regular group of travellers. This version covers the passenger app. The driver app and admin dashboard come in Version 2.

The Problem

Trotro fares rose with no warning, and drivers kept going on strike. Getting to class on time was never guaranteed. My school had two branches, Tesano and La Paz, so missing the one bus on your side could cost a whole morning. I surveyed 14 commuters (11 students, 3 workers). 13 named long waiting times. 13 just wait at the stop with no way to know when a bus is coming. 8 are often late because of transport. 9 would definitely prefer an institutional bus, and 11 would use one daily. The sample is small, so these are signals, not proof. I also asked 2 fleet managers how they run buses today. Both rated delay and breakdown alerts "very important". Both want the system to connect with the HR and payroll tools they already use.

The App

See how it works

Lign's passenger app does three things. Buy tickets in bulk and activate them as you travel. Hop on the nearest bus when you miss yours. Sign in with your institution's ID. A full walkthrough is coming soon.

Design Process

1.Research

  • Surveyed 14 commuters.
  • Asked 2 fleet managers how they run buses.

Small sample. Directional only.

2.Tickets

  • Students get money in lumps, not weekly.
  • So tickets are bought in bulk, activated one at a time and refunded if unused at semester end.

Activation screen still to design.

3.Ticket status

  • Each ticket is a coin.
  • Yellow "I" for inactive. Green "A" for active.
  • Long press to activate.

4.Hop-On

  • Missed your bus? Book Now opens Hop-On.
  • It shows the nearest bus on your side, seats left and ETA.

5.Institutional ID

  • One ID per school or company.
  • Student and teacher roles, so each group sees its own buses.

6.What I stopped chasing

  • Delay alerts as the headline feature.
  • Uber and Bolt already do ETAs well. The difference is the institutional ID, deferred tickets and Hop-On.

Driver routing around traffic is planned for Version 2.
