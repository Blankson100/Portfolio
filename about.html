I am a product designer from Accra, Ghana. My work rests on a single principle, which I call Fluency Without Learning: if anyone has to stop and work out how something works, my job is not yet done.

Much of what I design is for people in rural and low-literacy settings, where a product must make sense the very first time it is used. The projects on this site range from diagnosing crop disease for farmers to helping people manage diabetes and organising transport for schools and workplaces. I also design websites, this one among them, and write a little code, mostly with the help of AI.

Before my national service, I designed web and mobile products at Soia Finance, a fintech startup in Accra, working alongside its founders and engineers. I am now open to new roles, in Accra or remote.

Outside of work, I follow the Knicks, watch a great deal of anime, and remain undecided between Nolan and Scorsese as my favourite director. I am a little introverted by nature, though I am working on it. You can find me on Instagram and X, or write to me by email.
