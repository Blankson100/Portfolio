PlantAI: Crop Care

I grew up going to school in a farming community, so I've always thought about ways to bring what I do into agriculture. Technology in farming has always fascinated me. When a friend came to me to design a plant disease detection app, I saw it as the perfect opportunity. I expanded his concept into Plant AI: Crop Care, transforming it from a basic detector for tomato and maize into a comprehensive app for all plant types. The app uses computer vision for disease identification and language AI to deliver treatment recommendations in local languages based on the user's location. Research with farming communities taught me that simplicity builds trust, so I designed clean navigation, automatic language detection, and a shop finder that connects farmers directly to solutions.
