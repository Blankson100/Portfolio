The Challenge

Trotro strikes and unreliable public transit left students and workers stranded, with organizations having no digital tool to manage their own transport.

What I created

A managed transit platform that lets organizations book, track, and ticket their own fleet. Keeping their people moving regardless of public transport.

Lign

Lign is a smart transport platform built for organizations to manage their fleets digitally. Track your bus live, book seats, and never get left behind.
