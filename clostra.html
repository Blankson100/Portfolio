Clostra Platform

Sending money online, whether on Jumia or to someone selling phones at Circle, has always been a gamble. You pay, you wait, and sometimes you never hear back. I experienced it, watched it happen to others, and decided to build a solution. Clostra is an escrow-based platform that holds funds securely until both parties meet agreed conditions, so payments only release when a deal is done. It works across all kinds of transactions including physical goods, digital products, and services. An integrated AI assistant guides users through each step, paired with real-time tracking and a transparent transaction history. At its core, Clostra is about one thing: making transactions safer where trust isn't guaranteed.
