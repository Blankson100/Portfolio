Lign

Introduction

Lign is a multi-modal transport platform that helps schools, hospitals, and organizations manage their fleets digitally. It brings bus tracking, seat booking, bulk ticketing, and ride history into one app instead of scattered across phone calls and paper logs.

The goal was simple: give fleet managers real-time visibility into where every vehicle is, and give riders a reliable way to book a seat and know their ride is actually coming.

Whether it's a school running a daily shuttle or a hospital coordinating patient transport, Lign scales to fit how that organization already moves people.

The Problem

Most schools, hospitals, and small transport operators still manage fleets the manual way: spreadsheets, group chats, and phone calls to figure out which bus is where and who has a seat. That works until it doesn't, a missed pickup, a double-booked seat, a driver nobody can reach.

Riders have no way to see where their bus actually is, so they either show up early and wait, or miss it entirely. Fleet managers have no single source of truth, so every schedule change means more calls and more confusion.

What was missing was a shared system: one place for managers to see and control the fleet, and one place for riders to book and track their ride.
