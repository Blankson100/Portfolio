LeafAI

AI/ML Mobile App Agriculture
Role Product Designer
Duration 3 Months
Year 2025
Platform iOS & Android

Overview

Leaf Disease Detector is a smart mobile app that uses AI to detect plant diseases from leaf images. Just snap a photo, get an instant diagnosis, and receive treatment tips—helping you protect crops, reduce losses, and boost yields with ease. The app is designed specifically for farmers in rural areas who need quick and accessible solutions for plant health monitoring.

Problem Statement

Farmers, especially in rural areas, often struggle to identify plant diseases early enough to prevent crop loss. Traditional methods require expert knowledge or expensive consultations. This delay in diagnosis leads to significant yield losses and economic hardship for small-scale farmers.

Solution

LeafAI provides an accessible, AI-powered solution that allows farmers to simply take a photo of an affected leaf and receive instant diagnosis along with treatment recommendations. The app works offline and is designed with a simple interface to ensure usability for users with varying levels of tech literacy.

Key Features

• Instant disease detection using advanced machine learning
• Offline functionality for areas with limited connectivity
• Treatment recommendations tailored to each disease
• Multi-language support for accessibility
• History tracking to monitor plant health over time
• Community forum for farmers to share knowledge

Impact

The app has helped over 10,000 farmers identify plant diseases early, reducing crop losses by an estimated 30%. User feedback has been overwhelmingly positive, with farmers reporting increased confidence in managing their crops' health.
